# Knowledge Circulation in the Medieval Islamic Maghreb

## Research Project

This repository is being developed as a research database for studying the circulation of knowledge in the medieval Islamic Maghreb through Ibadi historical sources.

The project focuses on identifying and structuring information about scholars, places, texts, movements, and intellectual connections as documented in the sources.

## Scope

The database will initially be developed from selected Ibadi sources and may be expanded to include additional sources as the research progresses.

The initial phase is intended to support a research article on the circulation of knowledge in the medieval Islamic Maghreb.

## Research Objectives

- Identify scholars, places, texts, and intellectual connections.
- Document movements and relationships between different regions.
- Trace patterns of knowledge circulation through the evidence provided by the sources.
- Link structured data to their documentary sources.
- Prepare a dataset suitable for subsequent historical and analytical study.

## Methodological Approach

Historical information will be extracted from the sources, structured as research data, and documented with explicit references to the evidence from which each record is derived.

The database is intended to support historical criticism rather than replace it.

Initial development.
